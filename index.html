How to propose an open house

One straightforward intake, whether you're part of a partner office or reaching out for the first time.

STEP 01

Share contact details

First-time visitors tell us how to reach them. Assigned partner links can simplify repeat requests when connected to the private partner list.

STEP 02

Submit MLS numbers

Add up to 25 MLS numbers and share the listing stage, property readiness and timing preference.

STEP 03

Coordinate your open house

Requests go through immediately. Details are reviewed and any proposed hosting window is confirmed separately.
